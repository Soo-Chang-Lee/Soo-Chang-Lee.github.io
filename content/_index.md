I am a graduate student in physics, specializing in **High Energy Physics — Theory (HEP-th)**. My research interests lie at the intersection of quantum field theory, string theory, and the mathematical structures underlying fundamental physics.

I am particularly interested in:
- **Quantum Field Theory** — path integrals, renormalization, gauge theories
- **String Theory** — worldsheet dynamics, AdS/CFT correspondence, dualities
- **Mathematical Physics** — conformal field theory, topological aspects of QFT

This site is where I share notes, ideas, and occasional longer-form posts about topics in theoretical physics and mathematics that I find interesting.
